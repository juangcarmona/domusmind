## Architecture decisions and boundaries

These are applications of existing accepted decisions, not new accepted ADRs or claims of implementation. The [bounded context](product/model/domain/bounded-contexts/bc-household-assets.md) and [constraint](product/model/requirements/constraints/con-product-household-assets-boundaries.md) govern ownership.

1. **Keep Assets in the modular monolith.** Assets owns identity, Locations, obligations, occurrences and records; Household owns membership and authorization, Areas owns responsibility, and Calendar, Tasks and Lists own their respective work. Explicit references do not transfer ownership. This follows [ADR-0004](adr/0004-build-a-domain-centric-modular-monolith.md); separate services or a context per category add unneeded boundaries. Commands affect one aggregate, and cross-module collaboration follows [ADR-0008](adr/0008-collaborate-across-modules-through-persisted-domain-events.md); the exact aggregates and event contracts remain feature design work.
2. **Project due information rather than transfer obligation ownership.** Agenda is a read surface linking back to Assets, while explicitly linked work remains independently owned. Automatically creating work or synchronizing completion would contradict the execution boundary. Projection mechanism and consistency implementation are not selected here; test authorization and updates across the boundary.
3. **Calculate due state and retain finite outcomes.** Keep one pending cycle, and project future dates instead of generating an unbounded series. A completed cycle yields one visible outcome; corrections preserve the original. Exact persistence, transaction/concurrency strategy and correction presentation remain implementation choices subject to the accepted guarantees, not new product decisions.
4. **Keep first-release paperwork as authorized metadata and references.** A metadata grant is separate from source authorization; removing an association does not delete source content or history. A generic file store or binary upload is not assumed. A later upload capability requires separate decisions on secure storage, access, retention, deletion, backup and content validation.

ADR-0008's 2026-10-01 implementation note is historical, not current evidence: [EventLogWriter](../src/backend/DomusMind.Infrastructure/Events/EventLogWriter.cs) calls the dispatcher after saving when a dispatcher is present, and [ApplicationServices](../src/backend/DomusMind.Application/DependencyInjection/ApplicationServices.cs) registers handlers for `InboxProposalApproved`, `AssistantProposalRequested` and `ShoppingListRequested`. This does not establish an Assets event pipeline or settle its transaction design. Preserve the accepted ADR and its historical text.

## Lifecycle and structured-behaviour gaps

The owner inventory and searches of `docs/product/model/domain/lifecycles` and `docs/product/model/behaviours` found no Assets-specific LC or SB artifacts. Stateful subjects exist, so this is missing formal model coverage, not lifecycle non-applicability. Do not invent artifact IDs or transitions. Accepted prose establishes only the outcomes below; a full state catalog, initial/terminal states, allowed and forbidden transitions, guards and transition-linked behaviours remain to be modeled.

| Subject | Accepted prose outcomes | Unresolved coverage and dependent work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Asset | Register; ordinary removal with history archives; sold/archived history remains read-only. | Full lifecycle, sale/archive transitions, allowed operations and definitive deletion semantics need formal coverage; #357 and #359 must not assume unspecified rules. |
| Obligation | Define, suspend or cancel; prior history survives; active or resolvable due occurrence can receive an outcome. | Initial/terminal states, reactivation and operations in suspended/cancelled states are not fully specified; #358 needs a transition model. |
| Occurrence and history | One pending cycle can be overdue, completed, skipped or rescheduled; retries do not duplicate outcomes; corrections retain originals. | Formal transitions, guards and forbidden operations, including concurrent outcomes and correction effects on future due state, need coverage; #358/#359 must resolve missing observable semantics rather than infer them. |
| Document association and grants | Authorized metadata and references can be added or separately removed; source authorization remains independent. | Formal grant/revoke and removal transition coverage is absent; #360 must not infer unspecified lifecycle details. |

Existing accepted examples remain valid. Other calendar anchors and household timezone-change behavior are explicitly subject to product review in the recurrence rule. If dependent work needs an observable rule that the accepted model does not provide, stop that dependent behavior and route it through a validated, human-approved Product Change under [product authority](product/README.md#change-the-definition) before implementation; this map neither resolves nor approves those decisions. Formal LC/SB coverage should link each accepted transition to behaviours with their governing rules and executable tests; model coverage alone is not execution.
