## Problem

DomusMind has no canonical Product Definition. Product intent is spread across hand-written strategy, system, domain and context documents (now parked under `docs/_legacy/`), the OpenSpec capability specs and the domain code, and those sources contradict each other in places, for example on whether Meal Planning belongs to V1 or V2.

## Intended Product Outcome

A reviewable initial baseline under `docs/product/model/` that states who uses DomusMind, the household areas it covers, what each part of the product must do and the rules it enforces, in one consistent vocabulary. Every artifact is traceable to the evidence it was recovered from.

## Rationale

Recovered through a `prodshape recover` session over the legacy product, system, domain and context documents, the OpenSpec specs and the `recipe-management` change, with the domain code as corroboration only. Human decisions taken during recovery:

- Meal Planning is current product, to be refined in a later change (Q-0001).
- Recipes enter the baseline from the `recipe-management` change (Q-0002).
- Four actors: Household Member, Household Manager, Microsoft Outlook and the Calendar Sync Scheduler (Q-0003).
- The whole roadmap is modelled; V2 and V3 capabilities are low-confidence drafts (Q-0004).
- 57 further questions raised while reading the evidence were answered or deferred by the product owner (Q-0005 to Q-0061), recorded as evidence E-0158.

Product owner review decisions taken on 2026-09-30, after a product-engineer audit of the recovered draft:

- "review-merges": structural duplicates are merged. Assigning and transferring an Area's Owner are one use case (UC-AREAS-ASSIGN-OWNER); the Agenda day journey is part of JRN-PRODUCT-WHAT-MATTERS-TODAY and the inferred weekly-coordination journey is dropped; one rule (BR-AGENDA-PROJECTION-READ-ONLY) states that items projected into the Agenda are read-only there and owned by their source area, while each area keeps its own inclusion criteria; editing a person and editing one's own profile are one use case (UC-FAMILY-UPDATE-MEMBER-DETAILS); the Settings surface use case references the household settings and profile use cases instead of restating them.
- "roadmap-split": the phased roadmap (V1.1, V2, V3) and the low-confidence items that describe planned rather than current product are moved to the separate draft Product Change CHG-ROADMAP. CON-PRODUCT-PHASED-ROADMAP stays here as the pointer to it.

## Affected Product Areas

The whole product: household and members, areas of responsibility, plans, the Agenda and external calendars, tasks and routines, shared lists, and meal planning with recipes.

## Open Questions

Deferred by the product owner during recovery; each is recorded in the affected artifacts as "Undecided, deferred":

- Q-0011: what happens to tasks assigned to a person who is removed from the household (with member removal, V1.1, proposed in CHG-ROADMAP).
- Q-0018: what happens to the Areas a removed person owns or supports (with member removal, V1.1, proposed in CHG-ROADMAP).
- Q-0046: whether and through which channel reminders are delivered; until then a reminder is informational.
- Q-0053: how the Agenda's person scope behaves in a one-person household.
- Q-0057: how missed routine work is shown and whether routine occurrences can be completed.

Meal Planning is to be refined in its own Product Change (Q-0001): plan completion (Q-0033) and weekly templates (Q-0037) are the first candidates.

## Product Acceptance

A reviewer can read the Product Snapshot and recognise DomusMind: the household-first mission, the six areas of the product and their rules. Every remaining low-confidence (PRODUCT111) artifact, each describing current product, has been reviewed and either confirmed, corrected or removed. The roadmap is not reviewed as part of this change; it is accepted separately through CHG-ROADMAP.

## Out of Scope

Architecture, technical design, API contracts and persistence, which move to `docs/architecture/` and `docs/adr/` separately. Delivery status: this change says nothing about what is implemented, released or deployed. Future product: the phased roadmap (V1.1 hardening, V2 household domains, V3 intelligence and integrations) and the planned capabilities recovered with low confidence are proposed in CHG-ROADMAP, to be accepted only after this baseline is applied.
