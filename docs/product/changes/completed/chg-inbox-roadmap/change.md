## Problem

The phased roadmap places AI-assisted interpretation of household input in V3, while CHG-INBOX-ASSISTED-CAPTURE adds a review-first text Inbox to the current product scope. The roadmap must distinguish that bounded capability from later automation and integration work.

## Intended Product Outcome

Review-first, local interpretation of text Inbox notes into Plan and Task proposals is current product. The remaining V3 intelligence scope continues to include broader capture and integration capabilities, automation pipelines, projections and analytics; automatic execution remains excluded.

## Rationale

The accepted Inbox Product Change makes explicit per-proposal human review part of the current product. The roadmap should remain consistent with that change without moving autonomous agents or broader automation into current scope.

## Affected Product Areas

- Current Inbox capability.
- The boundary between review-first intelligence and future automation.

## Open Questions

None.

## Product Acceptance

- The roadmap identifies review-first local Inbox interpretation as current product.
- V3 continues to contain advanced intelligence and integrations, including autonomous execution and broader automation.

## Out of Scope

- Changes to the approved Inbox scope.
- Technical design, implementation and delivery.
