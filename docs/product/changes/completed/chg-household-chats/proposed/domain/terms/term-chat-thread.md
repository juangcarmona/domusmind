## Definition

A household-scoped conversation shared by its participants. A thread may be a household group conversation or a private conversation among selected household members.

## Distinguish From

- An Inbox item is captured input for review, not a conversation history.
- A household timeline is a projection of dated household records, not a message thread.

## Usage

Members use a thread to exchange messages and may explicitly invoke the assistant within it.
