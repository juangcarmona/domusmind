## Definition

A durable record of an actual cost incurred by the household, with an amount and currency, relevant date, description/category and optional same-household attribution such as an Asset, Member, Area or commitment cycle.

## Distinguish From

An Expense is not an estimate or future commitment. It may be recorded without a commitment. A Payment Record captures settlement state/date and must not silently duplicate the same cost in summaries.

## Usage

Use Expenses as the basis for actual-cost history and summaries. Corrections preserve the prior financial fact according to an approved audit policy. Do not treat payment reference, account balance or expense as evidence of bank reconciliation.
