## Definition

A household-defined expectation to pay on one or more future cycles, with a due-date recurrence, expected amount policy, currency and optional category, payee/provider, renewal/cancellation terms or contextual references. Examples include electricity, insurance premiums, school fees, property tax and subscriptions.

## Distinguish From

A Financial Commitment describes what is expected, not a completed Expense or Payment Record. A Calendar Plan is an appointment or time-bound activity; a Task is explicit work. Contract/subscription status and provider identity do not require separate aggregates for MVP.

## Usage

Keep effective-dated amount terms so price changes do not rewrite prior history. Project only a bounded upcoming horizon; retain historical due outcomes where a member records payment, cancellation, waiver or another explicit result. Cancellation stops future due projections without deleting prior terms or expenses.
