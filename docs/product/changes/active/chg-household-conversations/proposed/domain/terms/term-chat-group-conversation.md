## Definition

A named Chat thread for coordinated conversation among selected household participants, with a managed membership list and group-level identity.

## Distinguish From

- A private 1:1 thread has exactly two household members and no group administration.
- A household is the authorization and ownership boundary; a group does not cross household boundaries.
- An external messaging group is outside this term.

## Usage

Members use a group to coordinate, address other members or the assistant, share permitted content and read the history available to their current membership.
