## Definition

A file or media item made available through a Chat message to the authorized participants of its thread. An attachment may be uploaded for the conversation or reference an existing household-owned document when the source permissions remain valid.

## Distinguish From

- A household document is owned by its source product area; attaching a reference does not transfer ownership or widen source permissions.
- An Inbox item is not an attachment, even when it contains captured text.

## Usage

Attachment type, size, preview, retention and reference behavior are governed by accepted conversation rules and may not be inferred from the client UI alone.
