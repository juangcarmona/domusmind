## Definition

Content contributed by a household member to a Chat thread. A message is retained as part of that thread's ordered history and may contain text, attachments, mentions or a quoted reference to another message.

## Distinguish From

- An Inbox item is a separate capture record intended for interpretation and review.
- An assistant response is generated by DomusMind after explicit invocation and is distinguished from a member's message.
- A system entry records a conversation or membership event and is not a member-authored message.

## Usage

Members read and write messages only in threads they are allowed to access. A message may address the assistant or another participant and may be edited or withdrawn only under the conversation's traceability rules.
