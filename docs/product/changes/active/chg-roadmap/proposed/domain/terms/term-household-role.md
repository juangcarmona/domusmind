## Definition

Planned: V1.1. A person's permission level in the household, separate from their role: Manager (full management), Participant (tasks, calendar, areas), Observer (read only), Collaborator (limited to assigned scope) or Service (specific task categories defined by a manager) (observed: family-member-management.md, HouseholdRole).

## Distinguish From

- Would replace today's single Manager designation (TERM-MANAGER) with a scale of levels.
- Not the person role (TERM-MEMBER-ROLE); each role would allow only certain household roles (see BR-FAMILY-KIND-ROLE-COMPATIBILITY).

## Usage

Roadmap proposal only; not in the current product (observed: family-member-management.md, "roadmap proposals for V1.1 and later").
