## Definition

The explicit audience rule that determines which authorized household members may discover a Document's metadata and retrieve its content.

## Distinguish From

Household membership, document association, a member's relationship to the subject, or a manager role alone does not necessarily grant access to a restricted Document.

## Usage

The policy may make a Document household-shared or restrict it to explicitly permitted members, subject to product decisions about defaults, child-related records, grant administration and lifecycle changes. Unknown policy or unavailable authorization denies access.
