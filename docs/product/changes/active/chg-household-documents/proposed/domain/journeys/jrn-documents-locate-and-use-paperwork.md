## Intended Outcome

Household members can find current and historical paperwork at the moment it is needed while preserving private information and the ownership of related household records.

## Entry Conditions

A member has a household document to store or retrieve and has the necessary access to the household.

## Journey Narrative

A member adds the boiler warranty or car policy with a useful title and dates, deliberately chooses who can see it, and links it to its authorized Asset once that integration is available. Later, a member finds it from the library or the relevant household context, previews/downloads it, and can distinguish the current file from a prior version. When the policy renews, the member records the new validity period while preserving the prior policy. The expiry date alone creates no work.

## Variants and Branches

- A school authorization or identity certificate may be restricted to selected members rather than household-shared.
- A member can attach paperwork to an existing Person, Plan or Task only through supported same-household contracts.
- Asset associations are conditional on explicit alignment between Household Assets and Documents Product Changes.
- A file correction creates a new version; a substantively renewed document can supersede the previous logical Document.

## Completion Conditions

The member can locate and retrieve the intended permitted document, understand its current/superseded state, and reach it from a relevant authorized context without weakening another context's rules.
