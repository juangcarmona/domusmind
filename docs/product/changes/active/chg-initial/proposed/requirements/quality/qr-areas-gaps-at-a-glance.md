## Requirement

The Areas surface MUST let a household understand its ownership gaps in seconds, and assigning or changing ownership MUST be lightweight, without adding admin burden (observed: docs/_legacy/00_product/surfaces/areas.md, Success Criteria).

## Measurement

No threshold is stated by the sources. Proposed (inferred): in a moderated test, a member identifies every unowned Area in under 10 seconds, and assigns an Owner in no more than three interactions from the list.
