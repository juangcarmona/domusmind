## Definition

A cue shown only in the Agenda to make a coordination situation visible, such as "busy day", "school morning" or "trash day". It is derived from the household's plans, tasks and routines and is not something a person creates or stores (observed: docs/_legacy/03_domain/ubiquitous-language.md, Read-Model Concepts: Marker).

## Distinguish From

- Not a plan, task, routine or list item: it has no owner and cannot be edited (observed: ubiquitous-language.md, Marker "is not a domain entity").
- Not a label a person sets: markers come from existing state (inferred).

## Usage

Named only in the legacy vocabulary; no current specification defines which markers exist or when they appear (observed: absent from openspec/specs/web-app/spec.md and the Agenda specs). It supports the vision of seeing overloaded days early (observed: docs/_legacy/00_product/strategy.md, Vision) and stays as low-confidence future vocabulary (decided: Q-0061).
