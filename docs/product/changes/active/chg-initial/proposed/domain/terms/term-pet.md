## Definition

An animal that belongs to the household, such as a dog, cat or rabbit. Today a pet is recorded as a person with the Pet role (observed: openspec/specs/family/spec.md, Pet Registration; docs/_legacy/04_contexts/family.md, Pet).

## Distinguish From

- Not a person in the operational sense: a pet cannot have access, be assigned tasks, own areas, or appear in the Agenda's household rows and member timelines, but it may be a participant in a plan such as a vet appointment (observed: openspec family spec, Pet Registration).
- The legacy design keeps Pet as a distinct entity with its own identity, while the current product records it as a person with the Pet role (observed: openspec family spec, NOTE N6; family-member-management.md, "Keep Pet as a separate entity").

## Usage

Shown in the directory as its own group after adults and children (observed: openspec family spec, Pet Registration). Feeding schedules, vet tracking and pet care areas are planned: V2 or later (observed: family.md, Pet).
