## Definition

A durable historical fact about an Asset, such as maintenance, repair, inspection, installation, transfer or disposal, optionally linked to an obligation occurrence, provider, actual cost and supporting document.

## Distinguish From

A record may be created without a scheduled obligation. It is not an accounting ledger or an editable replacement for a Task or Calendar Plan.

## Usage

Preserve history and correction trace according to an explicit retention policy. Obligation completion should connect to the relevant history rather than produce duplicate entries.
