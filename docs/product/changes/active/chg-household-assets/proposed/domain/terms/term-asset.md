## Definition

A thing that a household owns or otherwise manages and wants to identify, locate, care for, document or retain history about. Asset is provisional internal language; user-facing terminology remains open.

## Distinguish From

An Asset is not an inventory quantity or stock item managed by Lists, nor does its presence imply commercial value, legal ownership, or that every item in a home must be registered.

## Usage

An Asset has household scope, identity and lifecycle. It may optionally reference a containing Asset, a physical location, a responsible Area/member, management basis, documents, obligations and records. These relationships have separate meanings.
