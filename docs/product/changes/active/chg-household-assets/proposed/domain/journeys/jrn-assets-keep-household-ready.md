## Intended Outcome

Household members share a trustworthy view of managed things, upcoming responsibilities, relevant paperwork and completed care.

## Entry Conditions

The household has a thing it wants to manage and at least one member can record or review it.

## Journey Narrative

A member registers a car, boiler or property, records useful details, optionally associates it with a containing property and a responsible member or Area, then records insurance, inspection, warranty or service obligations. Due information appears in the unified Agenda. Members can find the asset, open relevant paperwork, record an outcome and cost, and later review durable history and the next due date.

## Variants and Branches

- A second property can be an Asset with optional contained equipment, subject to validated terminology/model.
- An unscheduled repair or installation becomes a historical record without requiring an obligation.
- A task or appointment is created only by explicit member action in its owning context.

## Completion Conditions

The household can identify what it manages, the next due responsibility, its relevant owner/location and the evidence of prior work without duplicating another context's authority.
