## Requirement

Members can search and filter their household-managed things and reach relevant upcoming obligations and history using the existing compact list/inspector pattern, with equivalent interaction semantics on desktop and mobile.

## Measurement

Browser tests exercise registration, search, due navigation and history discovery at supported viewports; performance targets are set after representative household data volume is measured.
