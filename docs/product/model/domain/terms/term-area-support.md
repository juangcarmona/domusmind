## Definition

A person of the household who provides backup or shared accountability coverage for an Area. An Area may have any number of Support people, each listed once (observed: openspec/specs/areas/spec.md, Secondary Owner Assignment). Household-facing label: Support; domain label: Secondary Owner (observed: openspec/specs/areas/spec.md, Purpose).

## Distinguish From

- Owner: the single accountable person; adding Support leaves the Owner unchanged (observed: openspec/specs/areas/spec.md).
- Participant: someone involved in an Area without accountability (observed: docs/_legacy/04_contexts/responsibilities.md, Ubiquitous Language Notes).

## Usage

Shown on Area rows when present and managed inline from the Area detail (observed: docs/_legacy/00_product/surfaces/areas.md, Default View and Interaction).
