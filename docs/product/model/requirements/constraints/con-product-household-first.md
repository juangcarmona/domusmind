## Constraint

DomusMind organizes people, time, responsibilities, ongoing work and household-managed things around one shared household. Every product area describes that household's reality. Person-specific views remain within its shared context; the product does not provide a separate private productivity space.

The household defines membership, access and the data each area holds. Household Assets, including asset identity, obligations, documents and history, follows this boundary.

## Rationale

Running a household should not depend on one person remembering everything. Shared information helps people see what needs attention and who is responsible.

## Consequences

- Plans, Tasks, Routines, Lists, Areas, meal plans and Assets belong to a household, and shared views come first.
- A person's Agenda scope and assigned responsibilities are views within the household system.
- Product changes preserve shared household ownership, context and access rules.
