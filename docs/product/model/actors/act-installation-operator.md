## Purpose

The person authorized to initialize a new DomusMind installation.

## Goals

Configure the installation and designate its first administrator.

## Responsibilities

Completes the restricted first-run setup and enters the first administrator's email.

## Boundaries

Being the operator does not grant household membership or access to household information.
