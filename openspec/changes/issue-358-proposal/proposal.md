# Proposal

## Why

Households need reliable due dates and durable outcomes for asset obligations without fabricated missed cycles.

## What Changes

- Introduce one-time, monthly last-day and completion-relative obligations with one pending cycle.
- Support authorized lifecycle and occurrence actions with durable history, retry safety and concurrency protection.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `asset-obligations`: household asset obligation recurrence and occurrence lifecycle.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Household Assets domain, application contracts, persistence and API; no new user interface or independent work in other contexts.
